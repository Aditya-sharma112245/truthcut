# AI Collaboration Note

AI assistance is used as a proposal and review aid, not as an authority.

## Useful delegation

- Generate candidate audience promises from the story map.
- Suggest candidate scene orderings from a verified scene pool.
- Draft alternative truthful text cards.
- Identify possible implied spoiler or subtitle risks for auditor review.
- Draft documentation and test cases.

## Explicit checks against AI output

- Every scene ID and interval is checked against the episode manifest.
- Every claim is linked to story evidence.
- Rights and rating decisions come from structured rules, not model prose.
- Historical performance cannot override spoiler, safety, or rights constraints.
- Model-generated captions require alignment and review.
- Instructions embedded in scene descriptions are treated as untrusted data.

A plausible but wrong suggestion for this project would be to optimize CTR directly. That can select a viral spoiler or reproduce historical audience bias. TruthCut uses engagement only as a hypothesis feature after hard constraints and audience-specific rules.
