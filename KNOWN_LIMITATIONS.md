# Known Limitations and Human Decisions

## Known limitations

- The fixture uses a canonical manifest rather than performing full media analysis.
- A causal spoiler graph catches explicit and directly derived reveals, not every contextual leak.
- Semantic dialect review is represented as a human-review gate, not a solved linguistic model.
- Contract parsing assumes structured rights data; real legal language may require legal interpretation.
- Historical performance is not causal evidence.
- Replay mode proves reproducibility, not production-scale media throughput.
- The prototype emits an EDL and does not render a final master video.

## Human approval remains required for

- intentional spoilers;
- high-severity rating or safety ambiguity;
- actor likeness or promotional-rights exceptions;
- music approvals where the contract requires approval;
- dialect subtitle relationship changes;
- cultural framing and regional campaign claims;
- final text cards, voice-over, and publication.
